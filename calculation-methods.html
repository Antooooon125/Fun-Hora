How Fun-Hora uses XALEN

Fun-Hora is the Jyotish chart app. XALEN Ephemeris is the reusable calculation library behind it. In Lahiri mode, Fun-Hora runs XALEN in your browser to calculate planetary positions and speeds, the ascendant, and ayanamsa. Suriyayatra is selected by default and uses spreadsheet formulas for its natal positions and divisional charts.

  • Vargas Fun-Hora divides zodiac signs into smaller sections and uses the exact positions to place planets in divisional charts such as D9 and D60.
  • Jaimini Fun-Hora assigns roles such as Atmakaraka using the selected seven- or eight-karaka convention.
  • Vimshottari The Moon’s birth nakshatra determines the first dasha ruler. The Moon’s progress through that nakshatra determines how much of the first period remains.
  • Thai chart rules Fun-Hora draws the round and square Thai charts and displays Taksa roles and วิษะ markers.

Jaimini Rasi Drishti

Rasi Drishti is a sign-to-sign aspect. Each movable sign aspects the three fixed signs other than the next sign. Each fixed sign aspects the three movable signs other than the preceding sign. Each dual sign aspects the other three dual signs.

On a round wheel, turn on Rasi Drishti and choose a source sign to see its three outgoing arrows. The source starts at the sign containing that chart’s Lagna.

How Fun-Hora calculates Hora Lagna and Ghati Lagna

The Suriyayatra workbook method calculates sunrise and the Sun’s sidereal longitude using the workbook model. XALEN methods use XALEN’s sunrise and solar calculations. A birth before dawn uses the previous sunrise.

  • Hora Lagna (HL) adds 30° for each hour elapsed since sunrise.
  • Ghati Lagna (GL) adds 30° for each 24 minutes elapsed since sunrise.

Both calculations wrap around the 360° zodiac. The chart shows the reference sunrise in UTC. The workbook method rounds sunrise to a civil minute. XALEN uses apparent sunrise at sea level. Hora Lagna verses · Ghati Lagna verses

Vimshottari Pratyantardasha

Each Bhukti has nine Pratyantardashas. Each lasts the full Bhukti duration multiplied by its lord’s Vimshottari years and divided by 120. The first lord is the Bhukti lord, followed by the other eight in Vimshottari order.

Fun-Hora uses 365.2425 days per Vimshottari year and anchors the schedule to the birth date in UTC. If a Bhukti is already underway at birth, the table shows only the portions from birth onward.

D1 transits

Enter a date and time in the natal chart’s birthplace time zone. Fun-Hora calculates transiting planets and lunar nodes using the natal chart’s sidereal method and node settings, then places them by sign in the natal houses. The chart stays oriented to the natal Lagna.

The outer ring on round wheels and the T rows on square charts show transits. The exact positions table lists sidereal sign, degrees and minutes, and natal house.
